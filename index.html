ReelGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

ReelGuard ("the app") helps you notice how many short videos you scroll through and take breaks. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

ReelGuard is developed by Roshan. Contact: rr8512013659@gmail.com.

How the app works and the Accessibility Service

ReelGuard uses Android's Accessibility Service, only after you read an explanation in the app and tap "I agree" and then turn the service on yourself in Android Settings. It is used for one purpose: to notice when you scroll to the next video in the apps you choose (for example Instagram, TikTok or YouTube) so that it can keep a count and remind you to take a break.

To do this the service looks at:

  • scroll events and the size of the scrolled area,
  • the technical names of a few on-screen elements (used to tell a video feed apart from other screens),
  • which app is on screen, so it only counts in the apps you selected.

The service does not read your messages, passwords, posts, comments, captions or any other text, and it does not record your screen, take screenshots or perform actions on your behalf.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • today's reel count and the count for each app,
  • a daily history of counts for the last 30 days, used for the weekly recap,
  • your settings: reminder limit, which apps are monitored, the position and hidden/visible state of the floating counter, and whether you accepted the in-app explanation.

This information never leaves your device. We do not collect it, we cannot see it, and we do not share it.

Permissions

  • Accessibility Service: described above.
  • Notifications: to show your weekly recap on your phone. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Vibration: a short vibration when you long-press the floating counter.

The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

This version of the app contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. We do not sell or share your data. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy and the information in the app before the change takes effect.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can turn monitoring off in the ReelGuard settings or in Android Settings > Accessibility at any time.
  • You can delete all stored data by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > ReelGuard > Storage > Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

Children

ReelGuard is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
